The code in this repository is available under the [Functional Source License](../../LICENSE.md). The
name **Starlight** and the Starlight icon are not. They identify the app made and
signed by its developer, and the license doesn't give you the right to use them.

## You can

- Say your project is "based on Starlight" or "a fork of Starlight", in plain text.
- Link to this repository and to the official site.
- Show the name and icon when you write or talk about the official app.

## Please don't

- Call your build, fork or product "Starlight", or a name that could be confused with it.
- Use the Starlight icon, or one that looks like it, as your app's icon.
- Publish your build in an app store, website or download site as if it were the official app.
- Suggest that your project is made, endorsed or supported by Starlight's developer.

If you build Starlight from source for yourself, change nothing: this only matters once you share it.
If you're unsure whether something's fine, ask before you ship it.
